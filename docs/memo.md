# Memo: Reducing Missed Appointments

**To:** Clinic Operations Lead

**Data:** 110,521 appointments at public clinics in Vitória, Brazil, 2016

## Bottom line
Appointments booked further ahead are much more likely to be missed. Prioritize patients booked 8 or more days ahead for SMS reminders, and add phone calls for patients who have missed before.

## Findings
1. **Longer lead times are linked with more no-shows.** The no-show rate is 4.6% for same-day appointments and 33.0% for appointments booked 31+ days ahead.
2. **SMS is linked with fewer no-shows, mainly when booked further ahead.** With 1–7 days' lead time, the rates are about equal (23.8% with SMS vs 24.4% without). At 31+ days, the rate is 30.2% with SMS vs 37.5% without.
3. **No-shows are concentrated among repeat patients and some locations.** The 5.2% of patients with 2 or more no-shows account for 35.3% of all no-shows. Among clinics with 500+ appointments, Itararé stands out for both rate and volume: 26.3% on 3,514 appointments, vs 20.2% overall.

## Recommendations
1. **Prioritize SMS reminders for long lead times.** Start with appointments booked 8 or more days ahead, where the SMS gap is largest. (Findings 1 and 2)
2. **Add phone calls on top of SMS for repeat no-show patients.** Call patients with 2 or more earlier no-shows before their next appointment. (Finding 3)
3. **Start outreach in the highest-impact neighbourhoods.** Begin at Itararé, where a high rate and high volume combine. (Finding 3)

## Limitations
- The data covers only 6 weeks in 2016 and comes from one city in Brazil, so results may not apply elsewhere.
- These are correlations, not proof of cause. SMS reminders were not assigned at random, so the patients who received them may differ in other ways.
- We don't know whether patients received or read each SMS.
- With only 6 weeks of data, a patient's history is short, so repeat no-shows are likely undercounted.

## What to measure next
- Whether patients confirm receiving the SMS.
- The no-show rate in a controlled trial, comparing reminder and no-reminder groups at 8+ days' lead time.
